### 🎯 The Problem

Farm decisions often depend on fragmented information about:

- 🌱 Soil conditions
- 🌦️ Weather
- 🛰️ Satellite observations
- 💧 Water availability
- 🧪 Fertilizer requirements
- 📈 Market conditions

**FarmOptima brings these signals together into one decision-support system.**
